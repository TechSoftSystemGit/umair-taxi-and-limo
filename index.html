Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our taxi, airport runs, and Ireland tours.

Where is your taxi service based? ↓
We are proudly based in Youghal, County Cork. We provide local taxi service in Youghal and surrounding areas (Dungarvan, Killeagh, Castlemartyr, Midleton) as well as airport transfers to Cork Airport (ORK) and Dublin Airport (DUB), and private tours all around Ireland.
How do I meet my driver at Cork Airport? ↓
Your driver waits directly inside the main arrivals hall right past the baggage collection doors, displaying your name clearly on a sign or tablet, and will assist you with your luggage.
Are airport parking and road tolls included in the price? ↓
Yes, 100%. The price quoted during booking is completely fixed and all-inclusive. It covers all road tolls, airport entrance fees, parking charges, and 60 minutes free waiting time. No surge pricing ever applies.
How do private day tours work? ↓
Our private tours are inspired by the scenic itineraries of aboutirelandtaxitours.ie. You have exclusive private hire of the vehicle and a friendly local Irish driver-guide. You set the pace, stop for photos whenever you want, and enjoy Ireland without crowded tour bus schedules.
What payment methods do you accept? ↓
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Revolut), contactless phone payments, and cash.
