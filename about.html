About Umair Taxi and Limo

Rooted in Youghal, serving County Cork, Waterford, and travelers from around the world.

Umair Taxi and Limo is a dedicated passenger transport service based at Istanbul Bites, Upper Cork Hill, Youghal, Ireland. We were founded on three simple principles: absolute punctuality, honest communication, and genuine local courtesy.

While our company name includes "Limo", our primary mission is being your most dependable Airport Transfer specialist, your prompt Local Taxi service, and your trusted guide for Private Tours across Ireland. We do not hide behind aloof luxury terminology or inflated tariffs — we offer clean, comfortable vehicles driven by licensed local professionals who know the roads and care about your journey.
