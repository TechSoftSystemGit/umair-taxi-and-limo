# Umair Taxi and Limo brand guidelines

Umair Taxi and Limo is a Youghal-based airport transfer, local taxi and private tour business. Lead with Cork Airport transfers and make local journeys and Ireland tours easy to find.

## Logo

The horizontal wordmark combines an abstract road with the business name. Use the dark-background version in the header and footer and the light-background version on paper or light layouts. Use the standalone monogram in compact spaces and the icon for browser tabs.

- Dark and light wordmarks: `site/assets/logo/umair-dark.svg` and `site/assets/logo/umair-light.svg`
- Monogram and favicon: `site/assets/logo/umair-monogram.svg` and `site/assets/logo/umair-icon.svg`

## Colour and type

Use charcoal for navigation, warm ivory for reading surfaces and champagne gold for primary actions. The website uses Instrument Serif for display headings and DM Sans for body copy and controls. The colour values are defined in `site/assets/site.css`.

## Imagery and copy

Use south-coast Irish landscapes and clearly identified vehicle categories. Generated imagery is illustrative. Describe what passengers should provide when requesting a journey and confirm service availability, quotes and vehicle suitability by reply.
