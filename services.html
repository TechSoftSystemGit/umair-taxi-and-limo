Clarity & Focus

What We Don’t Do

We are an executive private chauffeur service, not a low-cost ride-hail app. Here is our commitment to standards.

✖ No Shared Shuttles or Stranger Rides

We never combine bookings. You and your party have exclusive private use of the executive vehicle for the entire journey.

✖ No Surge Pricing in Bad Weather or Rush Hour

Your price is fixed when you book. Rain, traffic on the M50, or flight delays will never increase your quoted rate.

✖ No Meters Running in Traffic

You will never watch an anxious meter ticking up in motorway delays. The agreed fixed price remains firm.

✖ No Unvetted or Casual Drivers

Every chauffeur is licensed by the National Transport Authority (NTA Ireland), fully insured, suited, and background-checked.
