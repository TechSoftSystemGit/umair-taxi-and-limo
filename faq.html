Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about airport pickups, local taxi dispatch, private Ireland tours, child seats, and payment methods.

✈️ Cork & Dublin Airport Transfers

Where do I meet my driver at Cork Airport? ↓
Your driver waits inside the main arrivals terminal directly past the luggage exit doors, holding a personalized nameboard. We assist with your bags and lead you directly to the vehicle.
What happens if my inbound flight is delayed? ↓
We track your flight number in real time via live radar. Whether your flight arrives early or is delayed, your driver automatically adjusts arrival time with zero extra delay charges.
How much waiting time is included for airport pickups? ↓
We provide a full 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time from the moment your aircraft touches down, giving you plenty of time to clear passport control and claim your luggage.

🚖 Local Taxi Service & Fixed Fares

What areas do you cover for local taxi service? ↓
We are based in Youghal, County Cork. We provide on-demand and pre-booked local taxi service across Youghal, Dungarvan, Killeagh, Castlemartyr, Midleton, and Cork City.
Are your prices fixed or metered? ↓
For airport transfers and inter-city routes, we provide transparent, guaranteed fixed prices per vehicle with road tolls and parking included. For short local trips, we offer honest standard fares. We never apply peak surge multipliers in bad weather or rush hour.
What payment methods do you accept? ↓
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Revolut), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash directly in the car.

☘️ Tours All Around Ireland

What tour destinations do you offer? ↓
Following the popular itineraries inspired by aboutirelandtaxitours.ie, we offer private day tours to Blarney Castle & Kinsale, Ring of Kerry, Copper Coast & Waterford Crystal, Midleton Jameson Distillery, and Cliffs of Moher, or fully custom bespoke routes.
What vehicles do you use for tours? ↓
Our private tours use spacious 7-8 seater tour vans (Mercedes V-Class & Ford Tourneo) featuring large panoramic windows for scenic views, comfortable seating, and ample room for bags.
Do you provide child and booster seats? ↓
Yes, free of charge upon request. We supply certified infant capsules, forward-facing toddler seats, and booster seats. Just let us know your child’s age when booking.
