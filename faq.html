Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about airport arrivals, flight tracking, luggage allowances, child seats, payment methods, and cancellations.

✈ Airport & Flight Tracking

Where do I meet my driver at Dublin Airport? ↓
Your chauffeur waits inside the main arrivals hall past the luggage carousels and customs exit doors. In Terminal 1, look near the arrivals information desk. In Terminal 2, look directly outside the glass exit doors in front of the sculpture/Spar area. Your driver will hold a personalized digital nameboard.
What happens if my inbound flight is delayed or lands early? ↓
We track your flight number in real time using official aviation radar. Whether your flight lands 30 minutes early or 3 hours late, your chauffeur adjusts automatically. There are no additional charges for flight delays.
How much waiting time is included for airport pickups? ↓
We provide a full 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time starting from the moment your plane physically touches down on the tarmac. This provides ample time to clear passport control and collect luggage.

🏷 Pricing, Tolls & Payments

Are tolls, parking fees, and VAT included in the price? ↓
Yes, 100%. All Dublin Airport entry/exit charges, Port Tunnel tolls, M50 electronic tolls, and Irish VAT (9% passenger transport) are fully included in the quoted price. You will never encounter unexpected fees.
What payment methods do you accept? ↓
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash directly in the car. For corporate accounts, we offer 30-day payment terms with consolidated invoicing.
Do your rates change during night hours or peak traffic? ↓
No. Our prices are strictly fixed 24 hours a day. Unlike ride-sharing apps, we do not employ surge multipliers for rain, Friday evening rush hour, or late-night arrivals.

🚘 Vehicles, Luggage & Safety

Do you provide child and baby safety seats? ↓
Yes, completely free of charge upon request. We supply EU-certified rear-facing infant capsules, forward-facing toddler seats, and booster seats. Simply inform us of your child's age when booking.
Can your vehicles carry golf bags and large luggage? ↓
Our Mercedes-Benz V-Class Extra Long MPVs easily carry up to 7 passengers and 7 full-sized golf travel cases plus standard carry-ons. For smaller parties of 1 to 3 with golf bags, our executive sedans provide ample boot capacity for 1 large golf case.
What is your cancellation and amendment policy? ↓
You can amend your travel time or cancel your transfer free of charge up to 12 hours before the scheduled pickup time. For corporate accounts, same-day adjustments are accommodated with dedicated dispatch flexibility.
